# Live Agile: A Practitioner's Guide

Welcome to **Live Agile**—a human-centered framework for navigating work, life, and long-term direction with clarity and compassion.

**Live Agile is a presence system, not a productivity system.**

This guide serves as a reference for practitioners, a facilitator's manual for leading Live Agile practices, and a knowledge base that will support AI-facilitated coaching within [Communi.Team](https://www.communi.team).

---

## What is Live Agile?

Live Agile brings together **seasonal intention**, **weekly rhythm**, and **daily awareness**, creating a structured yet gentle way of moving through complexity.

It's built on four temporal lenses:

- **The Wake**: The trail behind you—what actually happened
- **The Surface**: The reality of right now—capacity, energy, context
- **The Current**: The 3–5 meaningful movements for this week
- **The Horizon**: A sense of direction across 10–13 weeks

Together, these lenses create a system of presence and navigation.

**Read more**: [What is Live Agile?](1_Introduction/What_is_Live_Agile.md)

## Core Practices

### Weekly Review (30–60 min)
The heartbeat of Live Agile. A structured reflection where you:
1. Review what actually happened (The Wake)
2. Honour what moved
3. Acknowledge what resisted
4. Capture insights
5. Release what no longer serves
6. Choose 3–5 movements for the coming week

**See**: [Weekly Review](5_Practices_and_Ceremonies/Weekly_Review.md) for full details and facilitator scripts

### Season Planning (60–90 min)
Creating a 10–13 week horizon that includes:
- Context and season narrative
- 3–7 meaningful outcomes
- Story Threads (ongoing movements)
- Boundaries (what you won't do)

**See**: [Season Plan](3_Artefacts/Season_Plan.md) and [Example Season Plan](4_Templates/Example_Season_Plan.md)

### Daily Practice (5–10 min, optional)
A brief check-in with The Surface:
- What's my capacity today?
- Which movement(s) will I advance?
- What needs to shift?

**See**: [Daily Practice](5_Practices_and_Ceremonies/Daily_Practice.md)

## Philosophy

Live Agile reimagines agile principles through a lens of **presence and human sustainability**:

**Traditional Agile**:
- Velocity and delivery
- Sprint commitments
- Burndown charts
- Maximizing output

**Live Agile**:
- Awareness and alignment
- Weekly movements
- The Wake (learning from what happened)
- Meaningful progress

We draw from agile's iterative nature but center humanity over productivity.

**See**: [What is Live Agile?](1_Introduction/What_is_Live_Agile.md) for more on the philosophy

## Historical Note: Domain Language

Earlier iterations of Live Agile used different terminology (Vision Keeper, Team Guide, Goal Setting, etc.). This **Domain Language** is preserved in [wiki/Domain Language.md](wiki/Domain%20Language.md) for historical context, but the current framework uses The Wake, The Surface, The Current, and The Horizon.

**For new practitioners**: Use the current framework (sections 1–7). The Domain Language is being phased out.
