
# Getting Started with Live Agile

Welcome! This guide will help you begin your Live Agile practice in a practical, grounded way.

## Your First Week

### 1. Understand the Foundation (15 minutes)
Start by reading these two pages:
- **[Welcome](Welcome.md)** — understand the philosophy ("presence system, not productivity system")
- **[Core Components](../2_Framework_Overview/Core_Components.md)** — learn the four lenses (The Wake, The Surface, The Current, The Horizon)

**Key takeaway**: Live Agile is about navigating with awareness, not maximizing output.

### 2. Do Your First Weekly Review (30–45 minutes)
Even if you don't have a Season Plan yet, start with a Weekly Review:

Follow the structure in **[Weekly Review](../5_Practices_and_Ceremonies/Weekly_Review.md)**:
1. Arrive and breathe
2. Review the Wake (what happened last week)
3. Honour what moved
4. Acknowledge what resisted
5. Capture insights
6. Release what doesn't need to continue
7. Choose 3–5 movements for the coming week

**Tip**: For this first review, focus on the previous week. What actually happened? What did you learn?

### 3. Choose Your Movements (10 minutes)
For the week ahead, identify **3–5 meaningful movements**.

Remember: movements are not tasks. They're directions of effort.

**Examples**:
- ✅ "Establish foundation for authentication system"
- ✅ "Clarify next steps with design partner"
- ✅ "Complete draft of strategic proposal"
- ❌ "Write 10 unit tests" (too granular — that's a task)
- ❌ "Launch product" (too large — that's an outcome)

**See**: [Core Components](../2_Framework_Overview/Core_Components.md) for more examples of movements.

## Your First Season

After practicing weekly reviews for 2–3 weeks, you're ready to establish a Season Plan.

### 1. Review the Season Plan Structure (10 minutes)
Read:
- **[Season Plan (Artefact)](../3_Artefacts/Season_Plan.md)** — understand the purpose
- **[Season Plan Template](../4_Templates/Template_Season_Plan.md)** — see the structure

### 2. Create Your Season Plan (60–90 minutes)
Set aside dedicated time. Find a quiet space.

Work through each section of the template:

**Context** (10 min)
- What's happening in your life/work right now?
- What's the current reality?

**Season Narrative** (15 min)
- What's the story of this season?
- What wants to unfold over the next 10–13 weeks?

**Outcomes** (20 min)
- What 3–7 meaningful outcomes matter this season?
- These are destinations, not tasks

**Story Threads** (20 min)
- What ongoing movements will run through the season?
- How do they connect to your outcomes?

**Boundaries** (15 min)
- What will you intentionally NOT do this season?
- What temptations will you decline?

**Tip**: Don't aim for perfect. Your Season Plan will evolve as you move.

### 3. Connect Weekly to Seasonal
In each Weekly Review, ask:
- "Are my weekly movements aligned with my Season Plan?"
- "What's my Season Plan teaching me?"
- "Does anything need to shift?"

## Practice Paths

Choose the path that fits your context:

### Path A: Solo Practice (Most Common Starting Point)
**Week 1**: First Weekly Review (looking back at previous week)
**Week 2–3**: Continue Weekly Reviews, choose movements
**Week 4**: Create your first Season Plan
**Weeks 5+**: Weekly rhythm connected to seasonal direction

**Best for**: Individual contributors, freelancers, personal development

### Path B: Small Team Practice (2–5 people)
**Week 1**: Each person does individual Weekly Review
**Week 2**: Team shares insights from their reviews
**Week 3**: Team drafts shared Season Plan together
**Week 4+**: Weekly team reviews + individual movements

**Best for**: Small teams, partnerships, co-founders

### Path C: With a Facilitator
If you're being guided by a facilitator:
1. They'll lead you through Season Planning first
2. They'll facilitate weekly reviews
3. You'll establish personal rhythm over time

**Best for**: Team adoption, organizational rollout, coaching contexts

## Essential Questions

As you begin, these questions will guide you:

### Daily (The Surface)
*"What's true right now?"*
- What's my capacity today?
- What's the emotional weather?
- Which movement(s) will I advance?

### Weekly (The Current)
*"What moved this week? What did I learn?"*
- What actually happened vs. what I planned?
- What insights emerged?
- What 3–5 movements matter for next week?

### Seasonal (The Horizon)
*"Where am I heading? What story is unfolding?"*
- Are my outcomes still true?
- What patterns am I noticing?
- What needs to shift?

## Common Beginner Mistakes

### ❌ Starting with a Perfect Season Plan
**Instead**: Start with Weekly Reviews. Let your Season Plan emerge from practice.

### ❌ Treating Movements as Tasks
**Instead**: Think bigger. Each movement should feel meaningful, not administrative.

### ❌ Choosing Too Many Movements
**Instead**: Start with 3. You can always add more if you have capacity.

### ❌ Skipping Weekly Reviews
**Instead**: Protect this time. The rhythm is the practice.

### ❌ Being Rigid About the Plan
**Instead**: Your Season Plan should evolve. Reality informs the plan, not the other way around.

## Your First 90 Days

**Days 1–7**:
- Read Welcome and Core Components
- Complete your first Weekly Review
- Choose 3 movements for the week

**Days 8–21** (Weeks 2–3):
- Weekly reviews become routine
- Notice patterns in your movements
- Pay attention to The Surface (daily capacity)

**Days 22–30** (Week 4):
- Create your first Season Plan
- Connect weekly movements to seasonal outcomes
- Establish boundaries

**Days 31–90** (Weeks 5–13):
- Weekly rhythm is established
- Season Plan evolves with learning
- You're practicing Live Agile
