
# What is Live Agile?

Live Agile is a practice for working with awareness rather than urgency.  
It creates continuity between:
- **The Horizon** — seasonal direction.
- **The Current** — weekly movement.
- **The Surface** — daily truth.
- **The Wake** — honest reflection on what has already happened.

Where traditional agile focuses on projects, Live Agile focuses on **lived experience**, **meaningful movement**, and **sustainable change**.
