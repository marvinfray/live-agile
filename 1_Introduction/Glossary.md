
# Live Agile Glossary

This glossary defines key terms used throughout the Live Agile framework.

---

## Core Concepts

### Live Agile
A human-centered framework for navigating work, life, and long-term direction with clarity and compassion. It brings together seasonal intention, weekly rhythm, and daily awareness. **It is a presence system, not a productivity system.**

### Presence System
A framework focused on awareness, alignment, and sustainable movement—as opposed to a productivity system focused on output, velocity, and performance metrics.

---

## The Four Lenses

### The Wake
**The trail behind you—the story of what actually happened.**

Your source of truth for learning, pattern recognition, and honest assessment. The Wake is reviewed during Weekly Reviews and season transitions. It answers: "What actually happened? What did I learn?"

**See**: [Core Components](../2_Framework_Overview/Core_Components.md)

### The Surface
**The reality of right now—capacity, constraints, mood, energy, context.**

The present-moment truth that shapes what's possible. The Surface includes physical, mental, emotional, and contextual factors. Checking The Surface keeps your practice grounded in reality.

**See**: [Core Components](../2_Framework_Overview/Core_Components.md), [Daily Practice](../5_Practices_and_Ceremonies/Daily_Practice.md)

### The Current
**The 3–5 meaningful movements for the week.**

Your weekly focal points—chosen with care, aligned with your Season Plan, grounded in The Surface. These are not tasks but directions of meaningful progress.

**See**: [Core Components](../2_Framework_Overview/Core_Components.md)

### The Horizon
**A sense of direction across a 10–13 week cycle.**

The longer view, held in your Season Plan. The Horizon is not a rigid plan—it's a story that guides, adapts, and evolves. It provides direction without rigidity.

**See**: [Core Components](../2_Framework_Overview/Core_Components.md), [Season Plan](../3_Artefacts/Season_Plan.md)

## Key Artefacts

### Season Plan
A document that holds your seasonal intention, typically spanning 10–13 weeks. Contains: Context, Season Narrative, Outcomes, Story Threads, and Boundaries.

**See**: [Season Plan Artefact](../3_Artefacts/Season_Plan.md), [Example Season Plan](../4_Templates/Example_Season_Plan.md)

### Movement
A meaningful unit of progress toward your outcomes. Not a task (too granular) or an outcome (too large), but a direction of effort that can be completed or significantly advanced within a week.

**Examples**:
- ✅ "Establish foundation for authentication system"
- ✅ "Clarify visual direction through concept exploration"
- ❌ "Write tests" (too granular—that's a task)
- ❌ "Launch product" (too large—that's an outcome)

**See**: [Core Components](../2_Framework_Overview/Core_Components.md)

### Story Thread
An ongoing narrative or movement that runs through your season. Story Threads are different from Outcomes—they're the currents that support multiple outcomes.

**Example**: A Story Thread "Building infrastructure resilience" might support outcomes like "Deploy pipeline automation" and "Establish monitoring system."

**See**: [Core Components](../2_Framework_Overview/Core_Components.md)

### Outcomes
The 3–7 meaningful destinations in your Season Plan. These are what you're moving toward, but they're held lightly—learning and adaptation matter more than hitting fixed targets.

**See**: [Season Plan](../3_Artefacts/Season_Plan.md)

### Boundaries
What you will intentionally NOT do during a season. Boundaries protect your Season Plan from drift and overcommitment. They should be specific and name real temptations.

**Examples**:
- "No weekend work except emergencies"
- "No speaking engagements this season"
- "No new projects outside current commitments"

**See**: [Season Plan](../3_Artefacts/Season_Plan.md)

## Practices & Ceremonies

### Weekly Review
The heartbeat of Live Agile practice. A 30–60 minute structured reflection where you review The Wake, honor what moved, acknowledge what resisted, capture insights, release what no longer serves, and choose your movements for the coming week.

**See**: [Weekly Review](../5_Practices_and_Ceremonies/Weekly_Review.md)

### Daily Practice
An optional 5–10 minute check-in with The Surface. Helps you stay grounded in present-moment reality and choose daily focus. Also called "The Surface Check-In."

**See**: [Daily Practice](../5_Practices_and_Ceremonies/Daily_Practice.md)

### Season
A 10–13 week cycle. The timeframe is long enough for meaningful movement and short enough to maintain focus and adapt when needed. Seasons roughly align with quarters.

**See**: [FAQ](FAQ.md)

### Season Planning
The process of creating a Season Plan—typically done at the start of a season or during transitions. Can be done solo or with a team/facilitator.

**See**: [Season Plan](../3_Artefacts/Season_Plan.md)

## Key Concepts

### Gentle Carry-Forwards
Movements or intentions that didn't complete in one week and are carried into the next—but held lightly, not with shame or rigidity. The term "gentle" reminds us that incompletion is normal and provides feedback.

### Emotional Weather
The mood, emotional tone, or affective state that colors your experience. Part of The Surface. Emotional weather changes—it's something you observe, not something to fix.

**Example**: "My emotional weather today is anxious but grounded."

### Capacity
Your available energy, time, attention, and resources. Capacity is assessed as part of The Surface and varies day to day, week to week, season to season.

**Types**:
- **Physical capacity**: Energy, health, sleep
- **Mental capacity**: Focus, clarity, cognitive load
- **Emotional capacity**: Resilience, emotional reserves
- **Contextual capacity**: External demands and constraints

### Alignment
The felt sense that your movements, outcomes, and practices are coherent with your values, intentions, and reality. Alignment is assessed during Weekly Reviews and Season Planning.

### Rhythm
The regular cadence of practices—daily check-ins, weekly reviews, seasonal planning. Rhythm creates stability and makes the practice sustainable.

### Presence
The quality of being fully engaged with what's happening now, without performance or pretense. Presence is the foundation of Live Agile—being with reality as it is.

## Facilitation Terms

### Facilitator
Someone who guides a Live Agile practice—either for a team or for an individual. Facilitators hold space, ask questions, and support reflection without directing or advising.

**See**: [Weekly Review](../5_Practices_and_Ceremonies/Weekly_Review.md) for facilitator scripts

### Season Keeper
In team contexts, the person who holds the energy of The Horizon—maintaining the Season Plan, ensuring it's visible and referenced, noticing when it needs updating.

**Related**: In earlier iterations this was called "Vision Keeper." See [Domain Language](../wiki/Domain%20Language.md).

## Related to Traditional Frameworks

### How Live Agile Relates to Agile/Scrum

| Live Agile | Traditional Agile | Key Difference |
|------------|-------------------|----------------|
| Season | Quarter / Sprint | Emphasis on narrative and learning vs. delivery |
| The Current | Sprint Backlog | Movements vs. tasks |
| Weekly Review | Retrospective + Planning | Integrated reflection and planning |
| Outcomes | Goals / OKRs | Held lightly, adaptation-focused |
| Story Threads | Epics / Themes | Narrative vs. deliverable |
| The Surface | — | No direct equivalent (capacity awareness) |
| The Wake | — | No direct equivalent (pattern recognition) |

**Philosophy**: Live Agile reimagines agile principles through a lens of presence and human sustainability, rather than velocity and delivery.

**See**: [FAQ](FAQ.md) for detailed comparison

## Terminology Evolution

### Domain Language (Older Framework)
An earlier iteration of Live Agile used different terminology:
- Vision Keeper (now: Season Keeper, or no formal role)
- Team Guide (now: Facilitator)
- Goal Setting (now: Season Planning)
- Daily Check-In (now: The Surface Check-In / Daily Practice)

**Status**: The Domain Language is preserved for historical context but is being phased out in favor of the current framework (The Wake, The Surface, The Current, The Horizon).

**See**: [Domain Language](../wiki/Domain%20Language.md), [FAQ](FAQ.md)

## Common Phrases

### "What wants to move?"
A question asked during Weekly Review when choosing movements. It emphasizes listening to what has energy and relevance, rather than forcing productivity.

### "Check The Surface"
A reminder to assess present-moment reality (capacity, constraints, emotional weather) before making commitments or plans.

### "Hold it lightly"
An instruction to maintain intention without rigidity. Applied to Season Plans, movements, and outcomes—they guide but don't constrain.

### "Presence over productivity"
A core principle: the practice is about awareness and alignment, not about maximizing output.

### "Trust the rhythm"
A reminder that consistency matters more than perfection. The weekly and seasonal rhythm creates learning over time.
