
# Core Components of Live Agile

Live Agile is anchored in four temporal lenses. Together, they create a system of presence and navigation.

---

## 1. The Wake

**The trail behind you—the story of what actually happened.**

The Wake is your source of truth. Not what you planned, intended, or hoped would happen—but what actually occurred.

### Purpose
- Pattern recognition
- Learning and adaptation
- Honest assessment of capacity and impact
- Material for reflection

### In Practice
You engage with The Wake during:
- **Weekly Reviews**: "What actually moved this week?"
- **Season transitions**: "What did this season teach me?"
- **Decision-making**: "When have I seen this pattern before?"

### Questions The Wake Answers
- What actually happened?
- Where did my energy go?
- What patterns am I repeating?
- What did I learn?
- What surprised me?

### Example Wake Observations
> "I intended to focus on feature development this week, but actually spent 70% of my time in meetings and firefighting. The Wake shows me I'm overcommitted."

> "Looking at the past 4 weeks, every Tuesday I have high energy and deep focus. The Wake teaches me to protect Tuesdays for complex work."

> "This season, I completed 3 of my 5 outcomes. The Wake shows me I tend to overestimate what's possible in 12 weeks."

---

## 2. The Surface

**The reality of right now—capacity, constraints, mood, energy, context.**

The Surface keeps the system honest. It's the present-moment truth that shapes what's possible today, this week, this season.

### Elements of The Surface

**Physical**
- Energy levels
- Health status
- Sleep quality
- Physical environment

**Mental**
- Cognitive capacity
- Focus availability
- Clarity vs. overwhelm
- Competing priorities

**Emotional**
- Mood and emotional weather
- Motivation and resistance
- Stress levels
- Sense of alignment or dissonance

**Contextual**
- External demands (work, family, etc.)
- Team dynamics
- Organizational changes
- Life circumstances

### Purpose
- Ground planning in reality
- Enable sustainable pace
- Prevent burnout
- Support honest choice-making

### In Practice
You check The Surface:
- **Daily**: Brief morning check-in (see [Daily Practice](../5_Practices_and_Ceremonies/Daily_Practice.md))
- **Weekly**: During Weekly Review when choosing movements
- **Seasonally**: When planning or adjusting Season Plans

### Questions The Surface Answers
- What's my real capacity right now?
- What's the emotional weather today/this week?
- What constraints are active?
- What's demanding my attention?
- What's true that I might be ignoring?

### Example Surface Assessments
> "Surface today: Low capacity. Didn't sleep well, feeling scattered. Will focus on one simple movement rather than trying to advance three."

> "Surface this week: High stress from org changes. My emotional weather is anxious. I need to choose fewer movements and prioritize grounding activities."

> "Surface this season: New baby, unpredictable sleep, limited focus windows. My Season Plan needs to reflect this reality—fewer outcomes, more flexibility."

## 3. The Current

**The 3–5 meaningful movements for the week.**

The Current represents what's alive and moving right now. These are your weekly focal points—chosen with care, aligned with your season, grounded in The Surface.

### What Are Movements?

**Movements are NOT**:
- ❌ Tasks ("Write 5 unit tests")
- ❌ Outcomes ("Launch the product")
- ❌ Roles ("Be a good manager")
- ❌ Vague aspirations ("Work on marketing")

**Movements ARE**:
- ✅ Directions of meaningful progress
- ✅ Observable shifts that advance outcomes
- ✅ Completable (or significantly advanceable) within a week
- ✅ Connected to your Season Plan

### Why 3–5?

**Fewer than 3**: You might be thinking too broadly or avoiding commitment.

**More than 5**: You're likely fragmenting attention or listing tasks instead of movements.

The constraint helps you choose what truly matters.

### Examples of Well-Formed Movements

#### Technical Work
- ✅ "Establish foundation for authentication system"
- ✅ "Complete API integration with payment provider"
- ✅ "Resolve critical performance bottleneck in data pipeline"
- ✅ "Ship MVP of notification feature"
- ❌ "Write code" (too vague)
- ❌ "Fix bugs" (too vague—which bugs? why those?)
- ❌ "Finish entire redesign" (too large for one week)

#### Creative Work
- ✅ "Clarify visual direction through 3 concept explorations"
- ✅ "Complete first draft of strategic proposal"
- ✅ "Finalize brand voice guidelines"
- ❌ "Be creative" (not a movement)
- ❌ "Work on designs" (too vague)

#### Leadership/Strategy
- ✅ "Establish Q1 priorities with leadership team"
- ✅ "Clarify decision-making framework for product direction"
- ✅ "Strengthen relationship with key partner through alignment conversation"
- ✅ "Complete hiring process for engineering role"
- ❌ "Be a better leader" (not observable)
- ❌ "Improve team morale" (too vague and not a weekly movement)

#### Personal Development
- ✅ "Establish morning reflection practice"
- ✅ "Clarify career direction through journaling and conversation"
- ✅ "Complete learning module on system design"
- ❌ "Learn everything about React" (too large)
- ❌ "Be more mindful" (not a movement)

### Choosing Your Movements

Each week, ask:
1. **What wants to move?** (What has energy and relevance?)
2. **What aligns with my Season Plan?** (Does this advance my outcomes?)
3. **What's possible given The Surface?** (Is this realistic given my capacity?)
4. **What 3–5 movements matter most?** (What will I choose, and what will I decline?)

### In Practice
- Chosen during Weekly Review (see [Weekly Review](../5_Practices_and_Ceremonies/Weekly_Review.md))
- Guide daily focus (see [Daily Practice](../5_Practices_and_Ceremonies/Daily_Practice.md))
- Reviewed the following week: "What actually moved? What resisted?"

## 4. The Horizon

**A sense of direction across a 10–13 week cycle.**

The Horizon is not a rigid plan—it's a story that guides, adapts, and evolves as you move through the season.

### What The Horizon Contains

**Season Plan** (see [Season Plan Artefact](../3_Artefacts/Season_Plan.md))
- **Context**: What's happening in this domain right now?
- **Season Narrative**: What's the story of this season?
- **Outcomes**: 3–7 meaningful destinations
- **Story Threads**: Ongoing movements that run through the season
- **Boundaries**: What you will NOT do this season

### Purpose
- Provide direction without rigidity
- Create coherence across weeks
- Enable saying "no" to what doesn't align
- Hold the larger story while moving weekly

### The Horizon vs. Goals

Traditional goal-setting:
- Fixed targets
- Success = hitting the target
- Failure = missing the target
- Emphasis on control

The Horizon:
- Directional intention
- Success = learning and meaningful movement
- Failure = ignoring feedback and staying rigid
- Emphasis on adaptation

### Story Threads

**Story Threads are the ongoing narratives running through your season.**

They're different from outcomes:
- **Outcomes** = destinations ("Launch v2", "Hire 3 engineers")
- **Story Threads** = ongoing movements ("Building technical foundation", "Establishing partnerships", "Developing leadership capacity")

A single Story Thread might contribute to multiple outcomes.
A single outcome might be supported by multiple Story Threads.

### Examples of Story Threads

#### For a Product Team
- "Building scalable infrastructure"
- "Deepening customer understanding"
- "Establishing design system"
- "Growing engineering practice"

#### For an Individual Contributor
- "Developing strategic thinking"
- "Strengthening stakeholder relationships"
- "Mastering system design"
- "Building public presence"

#### For a Leader
- "Cultivating team trust"
- "Clarifying organizational direction"
- "Developing future leaders"
- "Building cross-functional alignment"

### Example Horizon Statements

> **Season Narrative**: "This season is about establishing sustainable foundation. After 6 months of rapid growth, we need to strengthen our systems, clarify our direction, and create space for the team to breathe."

> **Outcomes**: Scalable deployment pipeline, Team operating principles, Clear Q1-Q2 roadmap

> **Story Threads**: Building infrastructure resilience, Developing team practices, Clarifying product strategy

> **Boundaries**: No new feature development outside roadmap, No external speaking commitments, No weekend work except emergencies

## How The Four Lenses Work Together

### Daily
**The Surface** guides focus:
- "What's my capacity today?"
- "Which of my weekly movements will I advance?"

### Weekly
**The Wake** informs learning:
- "What actually happened last week?"

**The Current** guides action:
- "What 3–5 movements matter this week?"

**The Surface** grounds choices:
- "What's realistic given my capacity?"

**The Horizon** provides context:
- "Are these movements aligned with my Season Plan?"

### Seasonally
**The Wake** reveals patterns:
- "What did I learn over the past 10–13 weeks?"

**The Horizon** gets updated:
- "Does my Season Plan still reflect reality?"
- "What needs to shift for the next season?"

## Practitioner Examples

### Example 1: Solo Developer

**The Wake**: "Last week I completed the auth integration but got pulled into 3 urgent bugs. Pattern: Fridays are always chaotic."

**The Surface**: "Medium capacity this week. Good sleep, clear focus, but team is short-staffed so I'll have interruptions."

**The Current** (this week's movements):
1. "Complete user profile feature MVP"
2. "Establish testing approach for new API"
3. "Clarify deployment process with DevOps"

**The Horizon**: "This season (12 weeks): Ship user management system, Establish automated testing, Improve deploy confidence. Story Thread: 'Building deployment reliability' runs through all three outcomes."

### Example 2: Design Leader

**The Wake**: "Last 4 weeks: I've been reactive, not strategic. Too many 1-on-1s fixing immediate issues, not enough time shaping direction."

**The Surface**: "High capacity but fragmented attention. Need to protect focus time."

**The Current** (this week):
1. "Draft design principles for product direction"
2. "Establish weekly critique rhythm with team"
3. "Complete hiring plan for senior designer"

**The Horizon**: "This season: Clarify design strategy, Build design team capacity, Ship navigation redesign. Boundary: No project work—I'm leading, not doing."
