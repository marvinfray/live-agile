
# Core Components of Live Agile

Live Agile is anchored in four lenses:

## 1. The Wake
The trail behind you — the story of what actually happened.
A source of truth, growth, and pattern recognition.

## 2. The Surface
The reality of right now — capacity, constraints, mood, energy, context.
The Surface keeps the system honest.

## 3. The Current
The 3–5 meaningful movements for the week.
Chosen with care, aligned with the season, grounded in reality.

## 4. The Horizon
A sense of direction across a 10–13 week cycle.
Not a rigid plan — a story that guides, adapts, and evolves.
