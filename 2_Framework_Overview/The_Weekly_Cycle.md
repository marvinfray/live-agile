
# The Weekly Cycle

The Weekly Cycle is the anchor rhythm of Live Agile.

## 1. Review the Wake
- What moved?
- What resisted?
- What surprised you?
- What did you learn?

## 2. Notice the Surface
- Energy levels?
- Emotional weather?
- Hard constraints?
- Opportunities for ease?

## 3. Choose the Current
Select 3–5 movements that will shape the week.

## 4. Reconnect with the Horizon
Revisit seasonal direction lightly.

## 5. Close the Week
A gentle review:
- What mattered?
- What can be released?
- What wants to continue?
