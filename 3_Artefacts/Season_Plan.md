
# Season Plan (Horizon Artefact)

The Season Plan creates a 10–13 week arc of intention.

## Purpose
- Provide direction without rigidity.
- Hold the story threads that matter.
- Establish outcomes that shape movement.

## When to Use
- At the start of a season.
- During transitions.
- When weekly rhythm feels disconnected.

## Facilitator Notes
- Encourage narrative over task lists.
- Allow uncertainty; clarity emerges with movement.
