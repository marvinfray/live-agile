
# Weekly Review Practice

The Weekly Review is a moment of truth and integration—the heartbeat of Live Agile practice.

## Overview

**Duration**: 30–60 minutes
- Solo practice: 30–45 minutes
- Team practice: 45–90 minutes (depending on team size)

**Frequency**: Once per week, same day and time
- Common choices: Friday afternoon, Sunday evening, or Monday morning
- Consistency matters more than the specific day

**Context**: Solo or team
- **Solo**: Individual reflection and planning
- **Team**: Shared reflection, then individual movement selection

---

## Materials Needed

### Essential
- Your Season Plan (digital or printed)
- A way to capture notes (journal, document, or note-taking app)
- Previous week's movements (for reference)
- Quiet, uninterrupted space

### Optional but Helpful
- Timer (to maintain rhythm through sections)
- [Nested Goal Tracker](../tools/1077%20-%20Nested%20Goal%20Tracker%20%5BTemplate%5D.xlsx) (if tracking outcomes over time)
- Tea or water (create a small ritual)
- Facilitator if practicing with a team

---

## The Seven-Step Structure

### 1. Arrive and Breathe (5 minutes)
**Purpose**: Transition from doing to reflecting.

**Solo Practice**:
- Close other apps and notifications
- Take three deep breaths
- Set intention: "I'm here to see what's true"

**Team Practice**:
- Gather in your designated space (physical or virtual)
- Brief check-in: each person shares one word about their week
- Collective breath or moment of silence

**Facilitator Script**:
> "Let's arrive together. Close what you don't need open. Take a breath. We're here to witness the week with honesty and compassion. There's nothing to prove."

---

### 2. Review the Wake (10 minutes)
**Purpose**: See what actually happened, not what you planned.

**Questions to Ask**:
- What actually moved this week?
- Where did I spend my energy?
- What surprised me?
- What patterns do I notice?

**Solo Practice**:
- Review your calendar, commits, messages, or journal
- Notice gaps between intention and reality
- No judgment—just observation

**Team Practice**:
- Each person shares 1–2 minutes on their week
- Others listen without fixing or advising
- Notice themes across the team

**Facilitator Script**:
> "Look at the Wake—the trail you left this week. Not what you meant to do, but what actually happened. What moved? What took your energy? Name it without judgment."

---

### 3. Honour What Moved (5 minutes)
**Purpose**: Acknowledge progress and completion.

**Questions to Ask**:
- What did I complete or advance significantly?
- What felt aligned?
- What created momentum?

**Solo Practice**:
- Acknowledge each movement, even partial ones
- Notice what felt energizing
- Celebrate completions

**Team Practice**:
- Share what moved forward
- Team acknowledges each person's movement
- No need to justify or explain

**Facilitator Script**:
> "What actually moved forward? Name it. Even if it's smaller than you hoped, honour the movement. Progress isn't always dramatic—it's often quiet and steady."

---

### 4. Acknowledge What Resisted (5 minutes)
**Purpose**: See obstacles and patterns without shame.

**Questions to Ask**:
- What didn't move that I expected to?
- What got in the way?
- Where did I meet resistance (internal or external)?

**Solo Practice**:
- Name what resisted honestly
- Notice if resistance reveals truth (misalignment, capacity, external blockers)
- Resistance is feedback, not failure

**Team Practice**:
- Share what resisted
- Team validates the honesty (resistance is normal)
- No problem-solving yet—just witnessing

**Facilitator Script**:
> "What resisted? What didn't move? This isn't about blame—it's about seeing clearly. Resistance often has something to teach us. Name it and let it be."

---

### 5. Capture Insights (10 minutes)
**Purpose**: Harvest learning from the week.

**Questions to Ask**:
- What did I learn about my work, my capacity, or my context?
- What's becoming clearer?
- What assumptions were challenged?
- What patterns am I noticing over multiple weeks?

**Solo Practice**:
- Write down 2–3 key insights
- Notice if any insights inform your Season Plan
- Some weeks have profound insights; some don't—both are fine

**Team Practice**:
- Each person shares one insight
- Team notices connections between insights
- Capture themes for future reference

**Facilitator Script**:
> "What did this week teach you? About yourself, your work, your team, your context? Some weeks teach loudly; some teach quietly. What's becoming clearer?"

---

### 6. Release What Does Not Need to Continue (5 minutes)
**Purpose**: Let go of what's no longer serving you.

**Questions to Ask**:
- What commitments are no longer aligned?
- What can I stop doing?
- What's draining energy without purpose?

**Solo Practice**:
- Identify 1–3 things to release (tasks, commitments, Story Threads)
- This might feel uncomfortable—releasing is an act of courage
- Update your Season Plan if needed

**Team Practice**:
- Share what you're releasing
- Team supports the decision (no convincing someone to keep something)
- Collective release creates space

**Facilitator Script**:
> "What can you release? What no longer needs to continue? This is where we practice letting go. Release creates space for what matters."

---

### 7. Identify Gentle Carry-Forwards (10 minutes)
**Purpose**: Choose your 3–5 movements for the week ahead.

**Questions to Ask**:
- What 3–5 meaningful movements want to happen next week?
- Are these aligned with my Season Plan outcomes?
- Am I being honest about my capacity (The Surface)?
- What will I NOT do to protect these movements?

**Solo Practice**:
- Write down 3–5 movements (not tasks)
- Check alignment with Season Plan
- Notice if you're overcommitting—scale back if needed

**Team Practice**:
- Each person shares their movements
- Team checks: "Does this feel aligned and achievable?"
- Support each other in setting boundaries

**Facilitator Script**:
> "What 3–5 movements matter for next week? Choose from alignment, not obligation. Check The Surface—what's your real capacity? Name your movements and let them guide you."

## Full Facilitator Script (45–60 min Team Session)

### Opening (5 min)
> "Welcome. Let's arrive together. Close what you don't need. Take a breath.
> We're here to see what's true about this week—not to prove anything, fix anything, or perform. Just to witness with honesty and compassion.
> [Pause for collective breath]
> Let's begin."

### Review the Wake (10 min)
> "Look at your week. Not what you planned, but what actually happened. Where did your energy go? What moved? What surprised you?
> [2 minutes silence for individual reflection]
> Now, let's hear from each person. 1–2 minutes: What happened in your week? We're listening, not fixing. Just witnessing."
> [Each person shares]

### Honour What Moved (5 min)
> "What moved forward this week? Even if it's smaller than you hoped, name it. Progress isn't always dramatic.
> [1 minute silence]
> Let's go around. What moved?"
> [Each person shares briefly]

### Acknowledge What Resisted (5 min)
> "What resisted? What didn't move that you expected? No blame, no shame—just truth. Resistance teaches us.
> [1 minute silence]
> What resisted for you?"
> [Each person shares briefly]

### Capture Insights (10 min)
> "What did this week teach you? About your work, yourself, the team, the context? Some weeks teach loudly; some quietly.
> [2 minutes silence]
> What insights emerged?"
> [Each person shares one insight]

### Release What Does Not Need to Continue (5 min)
> "What can you let go of? What no longer serves? This takes courage. Release creates space.
> [1 minute silence]
> What are you releasing?"
> [Each person shares]

### Identify Gentle Carry-Forwards (15 min)
> "Now, the week ahead. What 3–5 movements matter? Check your Season Plan. Check your capacity—what's true about your Surface right now?
> [3 minutes individual planning]
> Let's share. Name your movements."
> [Each person shares their 3–5 movements]
> [Team offers brief reflections: "Does this feel aligned?"]

### Closing (5 min)
> "You've named your movements. They'll guide your week. Trust the rhythm.
> Any final reflections before we close?"
> [Brief space for closing thoughts]
> "Thank you for your presence. See you next week."

## Solo Practice Script (30–45 min)

Set a timer for each section to maintain rhythm:

**Arrive (5 min)**: Breathe. Close distractions. Set intention.

**Review the Wake (10 min)**: Look at your week. What happened? Write it down.

**Honour (3 min)**: What moved? Acknowledge it.

**Acknowledge (3 min)**: What resisted? Name it.

**Insights (7 min)**: What did you learn? Write 2–3 insights.

**Release (3 min)**: What can you let go of? Write it down.

**Carry-Forwards (10 min)**: Choose 3–5 movements for next week. Write them down. Check alignment with Season Plan.

---

## Common Variations

### Quick Review (15–20 min)
When time is tight:
1. Wake (5 min): What happened?
2. Learn (5 min): What's one key insight?
3. Forward (5 min): Choose 3 movements for next week

### Deep Review (90 min)
When transitioning between seasons or addressing complexity:
1. Extended Wake review (20 min)
2. Season Plan check-in (15 min): Is it still true?
3. Full seven-step structure (60 min)

### Async Team Review
For distributed teams:
1. Each person does solo review
2. Share in shared doc or async video
3. Brief sync call (30 min) to share insights and movements

## Troubleshooting

### "I keep missing my Weekly Review"
- Start smaller: 20 minutes instead of 45
- Anchor it to an existing habit (Friday shutdown ritual)
- Calendar block it and protect it fiercely
- Ask: what's the resistance teaching me?

### "I don't have much to reflect on"
- That's valid—some weeks are quiet
- Do the practice anyway (the rhythm matters)
- Ask: "What does this quiet teach me?"

### "My team rushes through it"
- Slow down the opening (spend more time arriving)
- Use timers to prevent one section from dominating
- Remind: this is a presence practice, not a productivity meeting

### "I feel shame about what didn't move"
- This is common—notice it with compassion
- Resistance and incompletion are feedback, not failure
- The practice is witnessing, not performing

---

## Weekly Review Principles

1. **Consistency over perfection**: Better to do a 20-minute review every week than a perfect 90-minute one monthly.
2. **Truth over performance**: Name what's real, not what sounds good.
3. **Presence over productivity**: The practice is about awareness, not optimization.
4. **Compassion over judgment**: Witness without shame.
5. **Rhythm over rigidity**: Adapt the practice to your context, but maintain the weekly rhythm.
