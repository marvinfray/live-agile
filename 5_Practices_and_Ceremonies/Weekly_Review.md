
# Weekly Review Practice

The Weekly Review is a moment of truth and integration.

## Structure
1. Arrive and breathe.
2. Review the Wake.
3. Honour what moved.
4. Acknowledge what resisted.
5. Capture insights.
6. Release what does not need to continue.
7. Identify gentle carry‑forwards.

## Facilitator Script (Example)
"Let’s take a moment to acknowledge the movement of this week, without judgement.
What changed? What learned you learned? What can be released?"
