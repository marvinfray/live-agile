
# Daily Practice: The Surface

While Live Agile emphasizes weekly rhythm over daily tracking, a brief daily practice keeps you connected to **The Surface**—the reality of right now.

## Philosophy

The daily practice is not about:
- Detailed status updates
- Productivity tracking
- Rigid commitments
- Performance metrics

It **is** about:
- Present-moment awareness
- Honest capacity assessment
- Gentle course correction
- Staying grounded

**Remember**: This is a presence practice, not a productivity system.

---

## The Surface Check-In (5–10 minutes)

### When
Choose a consistent time:
- **Morning** (most common): Before engaging with work
- **Midday**: During lunch or natural transition
- **Evening**: End-of-day reflection

Consistency matters more than the specific time.

### Format

#### Solo Practice (5 minutes)
Ask yourself these four questions:

**1. What's my capacity today?**
- Physical: energy, health, sleep quality
- Mental: clarity, focus, cognitive load
- Emotional: what's the emotional weather?
- Contextual: what's happening around me?

Be honest. High capacity and low capacity are both valid.

**2. What's alive in me today?**
- What has my attention?
- What wants to move?
- What feels urgent vs. important?

**3. Which movement(s) will I advance today?**
- Look at your 3–5 weekly movements
- Choose 1–2 to focus on today (not all of them)
- What meaningful progress can happen?

**4. What needs to shift?**
- What's getting in the way?
- What small adjustment would help?
- What can I release or postpone?

**Capture**: Write 2–3 sentences. That's enough.

---

#### Team Practice (10–15 minutes)
Also known as "Daily Stand-Up" in traditional frameworks, but with Live Agile language and tone.

**Structure**:
- Each person shares 2 minutes
- Others listen without problem-solving
- Total time: 10–15 minutes for teams of 3–7 people

**Each person answers**:
1. **Surface check**: "Here's where I am today" (capacity, mood, context)
2. **Yesterday's movement**: "Here's what moved yesterday"
3. **Today's focus**: "Here's what I'm advancing today" (1–2 movements, not all tasks)
4. **What I need** (optional): "Here's what would help" (support, information, space)

**Team responds**:
- Acknowledgment (not advice unless requested)
- Offers of support if relevant
- Coordination if needed

## Variations by Context

### For Individual Contributors
- **5-minute journal entry** each morning
- Note capacity, choose 1–2 movement focuses
- End of day: 2 sentences on what moved

### For Makers (Developers, Designers, Writers)
- Brief morning check-in before deep work
- Identify which movement gets focused time
- Protect deep work blocks based on capacity

### For Teams (Async)
- Post Surface check in shared channel
- Read others' check-ins
- Respond with support/acknowledgment
- No synchronous meeting required

### For Leaders/Managers
- Morning: personal Surface check
- Midday: team awareness check (reading others' updates)
- End of day: reflect on what moved across the team

## Sample Daily Practices

### Example 1: Solo Morning Practice

**Journal entry (5 min)**:
```
Surface: Capacity feels medium today—slept ok, but lots on my mind.
Emotional weather is anxious but grounded.

Alive: The authentication system redesign wants attention. Also feeling
pull to clarify partnership terms.

Today's focus: Advance "Establish auth foundation" (my weekly movement).
Specifically: finalize the approach and start scaffolding.

Shift: Need to postpone the meeting at 2pm if possible—I'll have better
focus in afternoon. Will ask to reschedule.
```

### Example 2: Team Daily Check-In (Async)

**Posted in team channel**:

**Alice**:
Surface: High capacity today, feeling clear.
Yesterday: Completed the user research synthesis.
Today: Advancing "Clarify design direction"—will share 3 visual explorations.
Need: Feedback from Bob on brand guidelines.

**Bob**:
Surface: Low capacity—sick kid, home with them today.
Yesterday: Reviewed Alice's research notes.
Today: Responding to Alice's request on brand guidelines (short task), then offline.
Need: Space to be offline most of the day.

**Carol**:
Surface: Medium capacity, context-switching between projects.
Yesterday: Shipped the payment flow fix.
Today: Advancing "Build checkout MVP"—focusing on form validation.
Need: Nothing blocking me.

## Integration with Weekly Practice

The daily practice feeds the weekly practice:

**Daily → Weekly**:
- Daily reflections inform your Weekly Review
- Patterns in capacity become visible over weeks
- Daily adjustments inform learning and insights

**Weekly → Daily**:
- Your 3–5 weekly movements guide daily focus
- Season Plan provides context for daily choices
- Weekly Reviews reset daily rhythm

**Think of it as**:
- **Daily**: Navigating the terrain (The Surface)
- **Weekly**: Seeing the path (The Current, The Wake)
- **Seasonal**: Knowing the direction (The Horizon)

## Common Questions

### Do I have to do a daily practice?
No. Many practitioners maintain a strong Live Agile practice with only Weekly Reviews and Season Planning.

The daily practice is helpful when:
- You want to stay grounded during complex weeks
- Your team needs coordination
- You tend to lose connection with your capacity
- You find it supportive

Skip it if it feels like overhead.

### What if I miss days?
Totally fine. This isn't a streak to maintain. If you miss 2–3 days, just resume. The Weekly Review will catch you.

### Can I do more detailed tracking?
You can, but ask why. If it's about:
- **Performance metrics** → probably not aligned with Live Agile
- **Genuine curiosity** → sure, if it serves learning
- **Client/team accountability** → consider lightweight tracking without losing presence

The question is: does it support presence or performance?

### Should I track my daily movements?
You can note what actually happened (useful for Weekly Review), but don't turn this into a productivity log.

Simple approach:
- Morning: "Today I'm focusing on X"
- Evening: "Today, Y actually moved"
- Weekly Review: look back at the pattern

---

## Daily Practice Principles

1. **Brevity over detail**: 5 minutes is enough. Don't let this become overhead.
2. **Honesty over optimism**: Name your real capacity, not your ideal capacity.
3. **Presence over productivity**: This is about awareness, not output.
4. **Flexibility over rigidity**: Some days you skip it. That's fine.
5. **Compassion over judgment**: Low capacity is not failure. It's information.

## Tools & Templates

### Minimal Daily Template (for journaling)

```
Date: [Today's date]
Surface: [Capacity, energy, emotional weather]
Focus: [1–2 movements from my weekly plan]
Shift: [What needs to adjust?]
```

### Team Daily Template (for async)

```
Surface: [Where I am today]
Yesterday: [What moved]
Today: [What I'm advancing]
Need: [What would help / what I need from team]
```

---

## When to Skip the Daily Practice

Skip it when:
- You're on vacation or taking time off (obviously)
- The weekly rhythm is enough for your context
- It starts feeling like performance theater
- It creates anxiety rather than grounding
- Your capacity is too low to even do the check-in

**The Weekly Review is non-negotiable. The daily practice is optional.**
